# Fundamentals of Higher Mathematics

My solutions for the [Fundamentals of Higher Mathematics](https://eulercircle.com/fundamentals-of-higher-mathematics/) course at Euler Circle. This was a three-quarter sequence (fall 2024 through spring 2025) covering the core foundations of higher mathematics: abstract algebra, linear algebra, real analysis, and point-set topology.

This course was a big step up from Transition to Proofs. Where Transitions taught me how to write proofs, Fundamentals taught me the language and structures that all of modern mathematics is built on -- from proving things purely from field axioms to working in abstract topological spaces.
