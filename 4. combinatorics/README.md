# Combinatorics

My solutions for the [Combinatorics](https://eulercircle.com/classes/combinatorics/) course at Euler Circle (fall 2025). This course went well beyond competition-style counting into the structural and algebraic side of combinatorics -- Stirling numbers, posets, generating functions, and more.

I also wrote an expository paper on hyperplane arrangements as a final project.

## Expository Paper

**Hyperplane Arrangements and Combinatorial Complexity** -- an introduction to the combinatorial and geometric structure of real hyperplane arrangements. Topics include:

- Regions, faces, and sign patterns
- Intersection posets and the characteristic polynomial
- Zaslavsky's theorem (counting regions via the Mobius function)
- Face posets and oriented sign-vector structure
- Connection to piecewise-linear functions in neural networks (single-layer ReLU networks define hyperplane arrangements whose regions correspond to the network's linear pieces)

I also gave a presentation on the paper (Nov 2025, in the `Hyperplane Arrangements Presentation` folder).
