# Transition to Proofs

My solutions for the [Transition to Proofs](https://eulercircle.com/classes/transition-to-proofs/) course at Euler Circle. This was a three-quarter sequence (fall 2023 through spring 2024) designed to teach proof-writing through progressively more advanced mathematical content.

This was my first proof-based math course. I went from writing informal arguments about logical equivalences to constructing epsilon-delta proofs and proving convergence of infinite series.

## Problem Sets

| File | Date | Topics |
|------|------|--------|

| Transitions 4 | Nov 2023 | GCD, Euclidean algorithm |
| Transitions 5 | Nov 2023 | Equivalence relations: reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, counterexamples for unions of equivalence relations |
| Transitions 6 | Nov 2023 | Modular arithmetic |
| Transitions 7 | Nov 2023 | Modular arithmetic, linear congruences |
| Transitions 8 | Nov 2023 | Fermat's Little Theorem (via binomial theorem), Wilson's Theorem, Chinese Remainder Theorem |
| Transitions 9 | Dec 2023 | Euler's theorem, primitive roots |
| Transitions 10 | Dec 2023 | Primes of the form a^2 + ab + b^2, sums of two and three squares, Brahmagupta--Fibonacci identity |
