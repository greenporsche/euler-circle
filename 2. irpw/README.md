# Independent Research and Paper Writing

My research paper from the [Independent Research and Paper Writing](https://eulercircle.com/classes/independent-research-and-paper-writing/) program at Euler Circle (summer 2024). This was an intensive 5-week program modeled after the University of Chicago REU, where I chose a topic, studied it in depth, and wrote an expository paper.

## Paper

**Stability Analysis in Dynamical Systems** (June 2024)

An introduction to stability analysis for linear and nonlinear dynamical systems, with an application to the cart-pole problem from control theory.

### Topics covered:
- Dynamical systems, state vectors, and differential equations
- Fixed points, stability, and asymptotic stability (epsilon-delta definitions)
- Eigenvalue analysis for linear systems
- Linearization of nonlinear systems via Jacobian matrices
- Dynamics and control: modifying system behavior to achieve stability
- The cart-pole problem (balancing an inverted pendulum on a moving cart)
- Future directions: Lyapunov's direct method

## Presentation

**Stability Analysis in Dynamical Systems** (July 2024) -- slides presenting the paper: fixed points and stability, the eigenvalue test for linear systems, linearization via Jacobians, feedback control, a spring-mass-damper example, and the linearized cart-pole system.
