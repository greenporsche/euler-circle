# Euler Circle Coursework

My problem set solutions and papers from courses at [Euler Circle](https://eulercircle.com/), spanning from fall 2023 to present. Each problem set and paper has its own folder with the LaTeX source and the compiled PDF, except Transitions 1–6, which weren't written in LaTeX and have only the PDF.

## Courses

### 1. [Transition to Proofs](1.%20transition-to-proofs/)
*Fall 2023 - Winter 2024 - Spring 2024 (30 problem sets)*

My first Euler Circle course. A three-quarter sequence that taught me how to write proofs, progressing from basic logic and induction through number theory and combinatorics to real analysis. 

### 2. [Independent Research and Paper Writing](2.%20irpw/)
*Summer 2024 (research paper + presentation)*

An intensive 5-week research program where I wrote an expository paper on stability analysis in dynamical systems, covering eigenvalue methods, linearization via Jacobian matrices, and the cart-pole problem from control theory.

### 3. [Fundamentals of Higher Mathematics](3.%20fundamentals-of-higher-mathematics/)
*Fall 2024 - Winter 2025 - Spring 2025 (30 problem sets)*

A year-long tour of the core areas of higher mathematics: abstract algebra and linear algebra in the fall, real analysis in the winter, and point-set topology in the spring.

### 4. [Combinatorics](4.%20combinatorics/)
*Fall 2025 (9 problem sets + expository paper and presentation)*

A deep dive into enumerative combinatorics, covering surjections, Stirling numbers, posets, generating functions, and more. I also wrote an expository paper on hyperplane arrangements and their connections to combinatorial complexity and neural networks.

### 5. [Infinite Series](5.%20infinite%20series/)
*Winter 2026 (9 problem sets + expository paper)*

Techniques for evaluating infinite series and products in closed form, going well beyond geometric and telescoping series into Fourier analysis, the Riemann zeta function, algebraic number theory, the Gamma function, continued fractions, and nested radicals. I also wrote an expository paper on Dobinski's formula for the Bell numbers.

### 6. [p-adic Analysis](6.%20p-adic-analysis/)
*Spring 2026 (9 problem sets + expository paper and presentation)*

Analysis and algebra over the p-adic numbers: p-adic expansions, Hensel's lemma, p-adic power series, Newton polygons, field extensions, and the profinite integers. I also wrote an expository paper on how 2-adic numbers describe feedback with carry shift registers in stream ciphers.

### 7. [Generating Functions](7.%20generating-functions/)
*Fall 2026, in progress (6 problem sets + paper summary)*

Generating functions as a language for counting: the symbolic method, exponential generating functions for labeled objects, Lagrange inversion for trees, and applications to probability. My paper summary previews an expository paper on the root degree of large random trees.

## About Euler Circle

[Euler Circle](https://eulercircle.com/) is a mathematics program that teaches proof-based mathematics to high school students. Courses are structured around weekly lectures and problem sessions, with problem sets due each week.
